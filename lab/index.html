Lab Live projects

Working tools, not screenshots.

Seven projects from the AI, agent and marketing-science side of my work. Each one runs in your browser on its own data or yours. Change the inputs and the numbers recompute.

01 Media mix model & budget optimizer Fits adstock and saturation curves to two years of weekly spend, then moves budget to where the next euro earns most. Open → 02 Geo-lift incrementality test Builds a synthetic control from untreated regions and tests whether a campaign caused the lift, with placebo-based significance and power planning. Open → 03 Intent-signal lead scorer ICP fit plus product-usage signals, every point traceable to a rule. Learns weights from outcomes and gates outreach copy before it sends. Open → 04 Brand & claims gate for AI-written copy Checks agent-drafted ad copy against voice, an approved-claims register, competitor and regulated-language rules. Pass, review, or block. Open → 05 CRM dedupe & standardiser Drop in a messy contact export. Get one clean record per person, every merge explained, nothing sent to a server. Open → 06 Multi-agent swarm with no orchestrator Replays a real run of the swarm. Every proposal decision is recomputed live from the audit log, and you can inject an objection to see the outcome change. Open → 07 Governance layer for marketing agents Every agent action lands in a ledger, gets checked, and runs, waits for a human, or is stopped. Approve or reject held actions and watch drift and the weekly report update. Open →
