Lab Live projects

Working tools, not screenshots.

Seven tools from my work on AI agents and marketing measurement. Each runs in your browser on sample data, and three take your own CSV. Change an input and the results update.

01 Media mix model & budget optimizer Which channels drive revenue, and where should the next euro go? Fits a model to two years of weekly spend, then suggests a better split of the same budget. Open → 02 Geo-lift incrementality test Did the campaign cause the sales, or would they have happened anyway? Compares test cities with a control built from the rest, and sizes the test before you run it. Open → 03 Intent-signal lead scorer Which accounts should sales call first, and why? Scores fit and product usage, shows the rule behind every point, and lets real conversions re-weight the model. Open → 04 Brand & claims gate for AI-written copy Can this AI-written ad go out? Checks it against brand voice, approved claims, competitor mentions and regulated wording, then passes, holds or blocks it. Open → 05 CRM dedupe & standardiser Which contacts are the same person? Turns a messy export into one clean record each, explains every merge, and keeps your file in your browser. Open → 06 Multi-agent swarm with no orchestrator How do agents agree on work with no one in charge? Step through a real run, see why each proposal passed, and add an objection to change the outcome. Open → 07 Governance layer for marketing agents How do you let marketing agents act without losing control? Every action is logged and checked, then runs, waits for approval or is blocked. Decide the held ones and watch the weekly report change. Open →
