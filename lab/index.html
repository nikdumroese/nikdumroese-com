Lab Live projects

Seven tools you can run.

They come from my work on AI agents and marketing measurement. Each one runs in your browser on sample data, and three also take your own CSV. Change an input and the results update.

01 Media mix model & budget optimizer Fits a media mix model to two years of weekly spend and revenue, then moves budget to the channels where the next euro earns most. Open → 02 Geo-lift incrementality test Tests whether a campaign caused its sales lift by comparing test cities with a control built from the rest. It also sizes the test before you run it. Open → 03 Lead scorer with product-usage signals Ranks accounts on fit and product usage, shows the rule behind every point, and lets real conversions re-weight the model. Open → 04 Brand & claims gate for AI-written copy Checks AI-written ad copy against brand voice, approved claims, competitor mentions and regulated wording, then passes, holds or blocks each draft. Open → 05 CRM dedupe & standardiser Turns a messy contact export into one clean record per person and explains every merge. Your file stays in your browser. Open → 06 Multi-agent swarm with no one in charge Replays a real run in which agents agree on work without a coordinator. See why each proposal passed, then add an objection and watch the outcome change. Open → 07 Governance layer for marketing agents Logs and checks every action a marketing agent takes, then records whether it would auto-approve, need review or be blocked. Decide the held ones and the weekly report updates. Open →
