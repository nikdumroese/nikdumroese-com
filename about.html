01 About

I build the machinery behind go-to-market.

I'm Niklaas. I build across go-to-market and AI: the frameworks, data, and agent systems that decide how teams execute, then automate the execution. I'd rather hand over a working system than a document about one.

I didn't start in engineering. Performance marketing was the most measurable corner of the field, and I kept going: six-figure ad budgets, incrementality frameworks in Python, a CDP end-to-end, then production multi-agent systems. Same instinct throughout: distrust the reported number, and build the system that produces an honest one.

At Pleo I've run AI & GTM operations: ops governance, data quality, and reporting, alongside a production AI layer that scales the team's output. Clean processes, trusted data, tools people actually use.
